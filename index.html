Government

A federal republic. The Constitution is the supreme law.

Executive Branch

President Neil Araña is head of state and head of government. Vice President Ghiriel Ballon assists the President and assumes presidential duties when the Constitution requires.

Legislative Branch: Federal Congress

Council of States: represents the federal regions. National Assembly: represents the people. Congress makes federal laws, approves budgets, oversees the executive, and ratifies major national decisions.

Judicial Branch

Constitutional Court: interprets the Constitution. Federal Supreme Court: the highest ordinary court. Federal, Regional, and Local Courts: handle national, regional, and municipal cases.

The Constitution

We, the people of Varelia, united by the Seven Beginnings, establish this Constitution to preserve freedom, uphold justice, advance knowledge, strengthen our nation, protect our people, and secure a lasting legacy for generations to come.
